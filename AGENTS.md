## Portfolio writing

- Write in Tatiana's first-person voice. Keep it clear, warm, specific, and conversational.
- Do not use em dashes in website copy. Prefer periods, commas, or a rewritten sentence.
- Avoid generic product-marketing and AI-sounding phrases. Explain the actual workflow, decision, and consequence.
- Use "I" for Tatiana's contribution and "we" for team work. Do not claim team outcomes as individual results.
- Preserve supplied facts and qualifiers. Do not invent metrics, customers, quotes, traction, or causality.
- In case studies, explain what changed, how it changed, and why it mattered. Distinguish proven outcomes from current hypotheses and learning.
